How it decides

Each role lists the competencies it needs, with weights. The score is the share of weighted competencies the engine finds evidence for. Mandatory criteria such as Class 12 are checked separately.
Strong match80% or more. Goes to the recruiter's shortlist.
Potential match50 to 79%. Short voice screen in the candidate's language.
UncertainUnder 50%, or a mandatory item not stated. One question, then a person reviews.
Fails a mandatory criterionRejected with the reason and a better-fit role.
This prototype uses a phrase dictionary in Hindi, Hinglish and English. In production it would be a language model trained on the bank's own role competencies; the triage rules stay the same.
